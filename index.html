Materials and Finishes
What We Build With

We build with surfaces that look right in fifteen years, not just today.

Every material selection is intentional. We specify premium materials not because they are the most expensive option, but because they are the right one for how the space ages, performs, and makes you feel every morning.

  • White Oak Cabinetry

    Custom-built, not stock. The grain stays beautiful for decades and resists warping and fading in ways that painted MDF simply cannot.

  • Quartzite and Quartz Countertops

    Brazilian quartzite for waterfall islands. Engineered quartz for perimeter runs. Both handle daily use without staining or etching over time.

  • Large-Format Porcelain and Custom Tile

    Fewer grout lines, cleaner surfaces, dramatically more elegant. Available in formats up to 48 by 48 inches for bathrooms and wet rooms.

  • Engineered Hardwood

    Dimensionally stable in Carolina humidity. Real wood surface with the same visual warmth and a far longer life expectancy than solid in this climate.
